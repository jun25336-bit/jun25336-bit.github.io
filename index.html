About Me

Hi! I am a master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan (Aug. 2025 – May 2027).

Before Michigan, I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

My research interests broadly include embodied AI safety, foundation models for robotics, and autonomous driving. I enjoy exploring different research directions and working with people from different fields.

I am currently seeking Ph.D. opportunities for Fall 2027. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaborating.

Project page | arXiv
